Sakina

Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

In short: Sakina does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps the following only in its private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your reading position, streak, daily goal and progress
  • Ayahs you save and ayahs you mark as memorised
  • Reflections (notes) you write on an ayah
  • Your settings, such as theme, reciter and text size
  • Copies of recitation audio and tafsir you have opened, so they work offline next time

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Content the app downloads

The Quran text and translation are built into the app. Some content is downloaded when you ask for it, directly from these public sources:

  • Recitation audio from EveryAyah.com, when you press play
  • Tafsir from the Quran.com API, when you open the Tafsir panel
  • Word audio from Quran.com, when you tap a word in Word by word

These requests contain only which ayah, word or tafsir to fetch. Like any internet request, they reveal your device's IP address to that service, which handles it under its own privacy policy. Sakina sends no identifiers, reading history or personal information with them. All requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Sharing

When you share an ayah as an image, the app creates the image on your phone and hands it to Android's share menu. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Permissions

  • Internet, to download the recitation, tafsir and word audio above
  • Foreground service (media playback) and audio settings, so recitation can keep playing with the screen off
  • Vibration, for gentle haptic feedback

Sakina does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Sakina is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
